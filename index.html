About Me

I am a Civil and Structural Engineer with an M.Sc. in Structural Engineering from Bahir Dar University. My research focuses on combining structural engineering knowledge with modern computational methods to develop transparent, reliable, and practically useful engineering solutions.


My current interests include explainable artificial intelligence, machine learning, structural health monitoring, bridge engineering, damage assessment, resilient infrastructure, physics-informed AI, uncertainty-aware prediction, and digital twins.

Featured MSc Research

Explainable Machine Learning for Predicting Shear Strength of SFRC Beams Without Transverse Reinforcement

My MSc research developed and evaluated machine learning models for predicting the shear capacity of steel fiber-reinforced concrete beams without transverse reinforcement.

Machine Learning XGBoost SHAP LIME PDP SFRC Structural Engineering Explainable AI

Research Methodology

Data

Experimental database containing more than 450 SFRC beam records and eight structural/material input variables.

Machine Learning

Linear Regression, SVM, Decision Tree, KNN, Random Forest, AdaBoost, Gradient Boosting, and XGBoost.

Explainability

SHAP, LIME, Partial Dependence Plots, and feature importance were used to interpret model behavior.

Engineering Interpretation

Machine learning findings are interpreted in relation to structural behavior, material properties, and established engineering knowledge.
